HOW TO PLAY

🚀 LAUNCH MECHANICS

• Aim & Power: Click and drag the creature backwards on the slingshot. Use the trajectory dots to guide your cosmic shot.

• Release: Let go of the mouse to fire through space!

• Trigger Powers: Click anywhere on the screen or press Space while your creature is flying to activate its special cosmic ability!

🌟 THE 4 SPACE BLASTERS

SOLAR
Supernova Burst: Triggers a high-impact thermal shockwave pushing and crushing nearby blocks!
FROST
Absolute Zero: Freezes structures into brittle ice, reducing friction to zero and causing collapses!
GRAVITY
Singularity: Spawns a mini black hole that sucks in blocks and enemies, then implodes!
BOUNCER
Hyper-Bounce: Gains an intense speed surge with extreme restitution to ricochet through defenses!
